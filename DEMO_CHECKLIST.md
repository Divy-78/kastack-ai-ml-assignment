1. Upload both assignment CSV files.
2. Show dataset structure only; do not expose sensitive values.
3. Show all six categories.
4. Show all 15 mandatory message IDs.
5. Show at least three tasks.
6. Show at least three events/meetings.
7. Show one unresolved/null field.
8. Show sensitive type, masked text, risk and recommended action.
9. Explain three classification decisions using the reason field.
10. Show one uncertain result and explain why.
11. Open src/pipeline.py and explain mask_sensitive() or build_outputs().
12. Explain limitations and possible improvements.
